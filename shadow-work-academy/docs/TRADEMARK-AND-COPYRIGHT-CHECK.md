# Trademark and copyright check: "The Shadow Work Academy"

Date: 2026-10-14. **I am not a lawyer and this is not legal advice or a clearance opinion.** I could not query the USPTO, EUIPO, WIPO or UK IPO directly (this environment cannot reach them, and it could not open Justia or the other sites below). Everything here comes from **web-search summaries of public pages**; some summaries contained inconsistent dates. Verify every item at the primary source before relying on it.

## Bottom line
**Treat the current name as high risk. Do not create the Gumroad product, buy ads, or launch under "The Shadow Work Academy" until a trademark attorney has reviewed this.** This corrects my earlier note in `LEGAL-AND-TRUST-REVIEW.md` §E, which said I had found no exact-name use: a fuller search did find one.

## 2. Copyright findings
* **The name and the domain are not protected by copyright.** Names, titles and short phrases generally can't be copyrighted, so copyright neither helps the other "Shadow Work Academy" nor blocks you. Trademark law (including unregistered rights) is the issue.
* **Course text:** written fresh for this project, with no copying from any source I used. I ran two spot searches on distinctive phrases and scenarios from the content ("The Apology Draft", "Quiet Scorekeeping", "three-second notice", the Module 5 weekend script) and found **no matching published text**. That is a spot check, not a plagiarism scan. Concepts like "shadow", triggers, boundaries and if–then plans are general ideas, which copyright does not protect.
* **Ownership/protectability of the text:** the course text was drafted by an AI assistant. As I understand current U.S. Copyright Office guidance, material generated by AI without sufficient human creative contribution may not be copyrightable, while human selection, arrangement, editing and original additions can be. Practical consequence: your legal ability to stop someone copying the text may be weaker than for human-written work. Mitigation to discuss with counsel: substantive human rewriting in your own voice, your own examples, and the terms of sale. This is worth deciding before you invest in the content as an asset.
* **Third-party material in the package:** fonts (Cormorant Garamond, Inter) are SIL Open Font License; the license files ship in `fonts/` and the ZIP. Screenshots are of this product only. The Cash Command OS and Rewired Mind logos/branding are not used. "Carl Jung" is referenced only descriptively.

## 3. What I recommend (your decisions)
1. **Pause** creating the Gumroad product and any spend until counsel responds. Cost of a rename is lowest now: there are no customers, ads or printed materials.
2. **Get an attorney clearance** covering: exact name "The Shadow Work Academy"; the term SHADOW WORK in class 41/9 (online courses, downloadable materials); the Isaia use; a risk opinion; and whether to approach Shadow Work Licensing or Isaia.
3. **Prepare a fallback name that does not use "Shadow Work" as the brand** and does not copy "Academy" naming. You can still describe the content as "a shadow work–informed self-reflection program" in plain descriptive text (counsel should confirm). Because your own brand and hub priority is Rewired Mind, a name that sits under it (for example a Rewired Mind–led name built around self-trust) would also build your own brand equity. I have **not** cleared any candidate; each needs its own search.
4. If you rename: the domain `shadowworkacademy.org` would also need to be dropped or redirected, and it may itself carry risk (it mirrors another business's name). Do not point the domain at a live site until counsel signs off.
