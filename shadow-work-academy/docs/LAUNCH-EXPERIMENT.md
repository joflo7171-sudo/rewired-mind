# Small launch experiment to validate demand at $197

Goal: learn, cheaply and honestly, whether your audience will pay for this program, at what price, and which message works. **Nothing here has been run, and nothing needs money except Gumroad's per-sale fees.** Thresholds below are my suggestions, not industry benchmarks.

## What we do not know
Whether anyone will pay $197 for a self-guided text/PDF program from Rewired Mind. The only Rewired Mind price visible to me is $9 (Drive sales-copy doc), so $197 is a large jump. Search interest in "shadow work" does not prove purchase intent.

## Setup (needs your approval)
1. Finish the P0 blockers in `LAUNCH-READINESS.md` (Gumroad product, test purchase, legal review, hosting).
2. Measurement without surprises: Gumroad's own product stats (views, checkout, sales, refunds) cost nothing and add nothing to the site. For site traffic and CTA clicks you would need analytics; I recommend a privacy-friendly option only after you approve it and the privacy policy is updated. Until then, give each source its own tagged link (`?utm_source=...`) so Gumroad/hosting logs can attribute visits.
3. Optional email capture for the free sample (Brevo form already exists on the quiz funnel) — needs approval and a policy update.

## Plan (3–4 weeks, $0 ad spend)
| Week | Action | Traffic source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Soft launch to your existing email list and quiz leads with the free Module 1 sample and the $197 offer. Two emails (sample first, then offer). | Rewired Mind list |
| 1–2 | 3 Shorts/TikToks/Reels on the program's specific scenes (e.g. "the one-word reply", "the quick favor") with a link to the page; 1 long-form video | YouTube/TikTok/Instagram |
| 2 | Review the numbers against the pre-set thresholds below. | — |
| 3–4 | If needed, run the one pre-planned change: price (see rule 2) **or** message, not both. | same |

## Metrics to record (weekly, real numbers only)
Visitors by source · sample downloads · CTA clicks / checkout starts (Gumroad) · purchases · revenue · refunds · support emails (what confused people) · revenue per visitor.

## Decision rules (set these *before* you look at results; adjust to your own tolerance)
1. **Enough data?** Wait for ~200 qualified visitors to the sales page before judging. Under that, the result is noise.
2. **Price signal.** If after ~200 visitors there are 0 purchases at $197, run the *same* page at a lower price (e.g. $67–$97) for the next ~200 visitors. That change is only honest if it is a real price change and the old price is not shown as a "discount".
3. **Message signal.** If sample downloads are healthy but checkouts are not, the offer or price is the issue. If clicks to checkout are low, the page/message is the issue. If checkouts start but purchases are low, look at checkout/price/fees.
4. **Refund/support signal.** Any refund reason or support email that repeats twice is a product fix.
5. **Go/no-go.** Define "success" in advance, for example "at least 3 purchases at or above $97 from the first 300 qualified visitors with no more than one refund". If met, invest in depth (audio/video, expanded lessons) and a bundle/upsell path. If not, the cheapest next step is a lower-priced entry version (Modules 1–2 plus the workbook) feeding the existing Rewired Mind funnel.

## Fit with Rewired Mind's funnel
Attention → trust → lead → first purchase → upsell. The Academy is best as a *premium* step after a lower-priced entry product, not as a first purchase from cold traffic. Suggested path: quiz or free reset (existing) → entry product (existing) → Academy. Keep cross-links between `shadowworkacademy.org` and `rewiredmind.org` so the hub still gets the traffic and list growth (your website rule).
