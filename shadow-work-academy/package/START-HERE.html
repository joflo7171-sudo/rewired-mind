What’s in this folder

  • Academy/app — the interactive six-module Academy (open index.html).
  • Academy/resources — the Complete Workbook and nine PDF resources: quick-start guide, self-worth exercises, trigger tracker, inner-critic worksheet, people-pleasing audit, boundary scripts, values and needs worksheet, weekly tracker, and self-trust action plan.

Using the Academy

  1. Open the Academy and begin with Module 1. One module a week is a comfortable pace.
  2. Your answers are saved in this browser on this device. They are not uploaded by the Academy.
  3. Use Progress → Download backup regularly. Clearing browser data or switching browsers will otherwise erase your answers.
  4. Keep this folder where you can find it. Bookmark the Academy page in your browser, or just reopen START-HERE.html.

This is an educational self-reflection program, not therapy or medical advice. Go at your own pace, and seek professional support when you need it. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.
