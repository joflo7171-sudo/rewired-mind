Get started in four steps

  1. Open the email from Gumroad (check spam or promotions if you don’t see it) and download the ZIP file.
  2. Unzip it. On Windows, right-click and choose Extract All. On Mac, double-click.
  3. Open the folder and double-click START-HERE.html (it opens in your browser), then choose Open the Academy. Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge all work.
  4. Begin with Module 1. Prefer paper? Print the workbook from the Resources folder.
Back up your answers. Your reflections are saved in your browser on this device. Use the Backup button on the Progress page now and then.
