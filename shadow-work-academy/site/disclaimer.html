Educational Disclaimer

The Shadow Work Academy is an educational self-reflection program created for general information and personal growth. It is not psychotherapy, counseling, medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Not a substitute for professional care

The Academy cannot replace care from a licensed mental health professional or physician. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. If you are experiencing persistent distress, or you have a history of trauma, abuse, or a mental health condition, please consider working with a qualified professional before or alongside this program.

Use it at your own pace

Reflection can bring up difficult feelings. You may skip, shorten, or pause any exercise at any time.

In an emergency

If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself or someone else, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line in your country immediately.

No guaranteed outcomes

Individual experiences vary, and we make no promise about specific results. Names and situations in the examples are illustrative scenarios and do not describe real customers.

Terminology

“Shadow” is used in a practical, everyday sense to describe patterns we tend not to look at. It draws loosely on ideas associated with Carl Jung, but the Academy is not a clinical Jungian program and does not claim scientific validation of any specific technique.
