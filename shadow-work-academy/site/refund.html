Refund Policy

We want you to buy the Academy with confidence. If it is not right for you, you can ask for a refund.

30-day refund

If you contact us within 30 days of your purchase date at support@rewiredmind.org, we will refund the full purchase price. You do not need to explain why, although feedback helps us improve.

How to request a refund

Email support@rewiredmind.org from the address you used to purchase, or include your order number. Refunds are issued to your original payment method through our checkout provider, and your bank may take several business days to show the credit.

Digital products

Because the Academy is a digital download, access cannot be returned. Upon refund we ask that you stop using and delete your copy of the files. Refund requests after 30 days are considered at our discretion.

Problems with your download

If you have trouble downloading or opening the files, contact support first and we will help or replace the files.
