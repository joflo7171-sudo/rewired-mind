Privacy Policy

The short version

The interactive Academy stores what you write in your own browser on your own device. The Academy files do not send your reflections to us or to anyone else.

This website

This sales website may load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google’s servers when you visit. If analytics or advertising tools are added to this site, they will be disclosed here before use.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Gumroad. Gumroad collects the information needed to process your payment and deliver your download (such as name, email address, and payment details) under its own privacy policy. We receive order information such as your email address and purchase details so that we can provide support and deliver the product.

Support emails

If you email us, we use the information you send to respond and keep a record of the conversation.

The Academy application

  • Your answers, progress and name (if you enter one) are stored using your browser’s local storage on your device.
  • They are not uploaded by the Academy. Anyone with access to your device and browser profile could see them.
  • Clearing site data, using private browsing, or switching browsers or devices will remove or hide them. Use the backup feature to keep a copy.
  • When you are online, the Academy loads fonts from Google Fonts, which involves a request to Google. It sends no reflections.
  • Backup files you download contain everything you wrote. Store them privately.

Your choices

You can erase Academy data at any time from the Progress page. To ask about information we hold about you, contact support@rewiredmind.org.

Children

The Academy is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18.
