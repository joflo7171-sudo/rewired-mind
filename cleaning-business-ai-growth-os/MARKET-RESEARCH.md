# Market Research: Digital Products for Cleaning-Business Owners

**Research date:** 2026-09-30
**Scope:** Templates, spreadsheets, Notion systems, bundles and low-end SaaS sold to residential, commercial, maid-service and Airbnb-turnover cleaning businesses.

---

## 1. Methodology and limits of verification

**How the data was collected**
- Web searches (US results) across Etsy, Gumroad, Notion Marketplace (notion.com/templates), Creative Market, Payhip, Stan Store, independent sites (Rescue My Maid Service, Cleaning Company Blueprint, digitalbizplr), Template.net, and SaaS pricing roundups for Jobber, Housecall Pro, ZenMaid, Launch27, BookingKoala, Swept and QuickBooks Online.
- Nothing was bought, signed up for, or posted.

**Important limitation: no pages could be opened directly.**
Every direct page fetch was refused by this environment's network egress proxy. That includes Etsy, Gumroad subdomains, notion.com and getjobber.com. **Every figure below comes from search-engine result snippets and summaries. None was checked on the live listing page.** Treat all prices, review counts and feature lists as "from search snippet, not verified on page" unless a note says otherwise.

Specific caveats:
- Etsy prices change constantly because of rolling "sales" (25–70% off is normal). Snippets for the same listing sometimes disagree. For example, one CRM tracker showed as both $12.54 and $12.44. Treat these prices as a snapshot.
- Review counts on Etsy snippets can be **shop-level rather than listing-level**. Where that is ambiguous, it is marked.
- Some Notion Marketplace prices came from search metadata that contradicted itself. The "Cleaning Business Command Center" snippet said both Free and $199. These are marked "could not verify".
- SaaS prices come from third-party 2026 pricing roundups (Capterra, costbench, cleanbizsoftware and others), not from the vendors' own pages. They are consistent across several sources but not vendor-verified.
- No product, price or review count has been invented. Where a figure was not found, the cell says "not found".

**Legend for the Verification column:** S = from search snippet, not verified on page · S? = snippet was ambiguous or contradicted another snippet · NF = not found.

## 4. Per-category findings

### 4.1 "Business in a box" / operating-system bundles
- **What exists:** Two very different things share this label:
  1. **Canva document dumps** such as Etsy 180–300 page bundles, Creative Market's 126-page kit and the PLR bundle. These sell for **$10–$75**.
  2. **Education-led bundles** such as CleanVantage ($297), Bundly ($199) and CleaningLaunch ($1,941). These are mostly ebooks, roadmaps and courses, not working tools.
- **Observation:** None of the "box" bundles found has a real, connected operating spreadsheet. The "financial spreadsheet" is usually a single tab thrown in. The only review signal found on a 300-page bundle was **2.0★ from 8 reviews** (S?), which suggests the page count sells the product but does not keep buyers happy.
- **PLR contamination:** digitalbizplr sells a 70+ template cleaning bundle with **resell rights for $17**. Many cheap Etsy bundles are therefore likely rebranded PLR, which means the same content circulates under many storefronts.

### 4.2 CRMs / client management
- **Spreadsheet CRMs** on Etsy cost **$5–$17**. They offer client lists, appointment and payment logs, and simple dashboards counting current, former and potential clients.
- **Notion CRMs** range from **free** (Cleaning CRM Lite, Notionallity OS), used as lead magnets, up to $59–$199 when bundled into an "OS".
- **Gap:** Few of these tie a client to a recurring schedule, a price history or profitability per client. They are contact lists with status columns.

### 4.3 Pricing calculators / quote systems
- **This is the most active category** and has the widest spread of prices:
  - Etsy residential calculators cost **$4–$18**. The best seller, Clean With Carly, uses **fixed national-average formulas that are not customizable** and handles one add-on at a time.
  - SimplyPMA (~$8) is more serious. It models labor, supplies, tax, markup and margin, and produces a quote.
  - **Commercial specialists charge $99–$169** (CleanBidsPro $99, QuickCal $169). They justify the price with loaded labor cost, overhead allocation, risk factors and per-visit or per-month pricing, where underbidding directly loses money.
- **Takeaway:** Buyers pay 10x more when the calculator protects margin, meaning it uses loaded labor and overhead, compared with one that only looks up a price.

### 4.4 Bookkeeping / P&L / job-tracking spreadsheets
- The typical product is **$2–$19**: a checkbook tab plus an auto-updating P&L, often in Excel with a Google Sheets link (e.g. $6.00 listing).
- A few listings claim larger workbooks. One snippet described a "33 sheets / 3,800+ formulas" pricing, quote and profit dashboard (price and seller NF).
- They usually do not connect to quotes or jobs. Income is typed in by hand instead of flowing from completed jobs.

### 4.5 Notion systems
- There is a clear tier of **$59 (Service Cleaner OS), $99 (Cleaning Company OS by Mehdi) and $199 (Cleaning Company OS original; Nation4Business)**, alongside free lead-magnet templates.
- **Strengths:** relational databases (clients ↔ jobs ↔ staff), good-looking dashboards, flexible views.
- **Weaknesses:** Notion formulas are weak for real pricing and margin math. Cleaners tend to live in their phones and texts, not in Notion. There is no offline use, and the learning curve is steep for non-technical owners. None of the listings mentions recurring-job generation or QC scoring.

### 4.6 Template bundles (forms, checklists, flyers, contracts)
- The Etsy norm is **$5–$20** for 100–300 Canva pages. Creative Market kits run about **$75 with a commercial license**. Independent SOP and compliance packs run **$32–$96** (Rescue My Maid Service). Operator-made PDFs run **$38–$48 each / $99 bundle** (Mad Clean).
- **Canva-only is dominant.** Contracts and agreements appear in almost every bundle, but the snippets show no mention of attorney review or jurisdiction.

## 5. Synthesis

### 5.1 Price bands observed

| Band | What's typical | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Under $20** | Most of Etsy. Single-purpose Google Sheets (CRM, P&L, simple calculator) and 100–300 page Canva bundles, nearly always shown "on sale". Includes PLR. | Client tracker $5.20; P&L $6; pricing calc ~$8; 180-page bundle $10.95; CRM $17; PLR bundle $17 |
| **$20–$50** | Multi-template packs from operators and consultants, individual SOP bundles, single-topic operator guides | cleaningclass101 40+ templates $24.99; RMMS bundles $32/$40; Mad Clean guides $37.99–$47.99 |
| **$50–$100** | Notion OS entry tier, Notion "Pro" command centers, commercial pricing tools, Creative Market commercial licenses, operator bundles | Service Cleaner OS $59; Command Center Pro $59.98; Creative Market commercial $75; RMMS compliance $96; Mad Clean bundle $99; CleanBidsPro $99; Mehdi OS $99 |
| **$100–$300** | Premium Notion OSes, specialist commercial calculators, education-plus-template bundles | QuickCal $169; Cleaning Company OS $199; Nation4Business OS $199; Bundly $199; CleanVantage $297 |
| (Above) | Courses and coaching | Coaching $250/hr; CleaningLaunch course $1,941 |

### 5.2 Common strengths
- Cheap and instant, with no subscription. Most sellers use "one-time purchase, no monthly fees" as their main message.
- Google Sheets plus Excel compatibility is standard for spreadsheet products.
- Visual polish: Canva-editable branding, A4 and Letter sizes, dashboards with charts.
- Cleaning-specific wording: move-in/out, deep clean, Airbnb/STR, and commercial variants all appear.
- Operator credibility sells. Clean With Carly (5.0★, 342 shop reviews, 4,400+ sales) and the operator-coaches win on trust rather than on how deep the features go.

### 5.3 Common weaknesses
- **Disconnected tools:** The CRM, calculator, P&L and job tracker are sold as separate files, often by separate sellers. A quote does not become a job, and a job does not become income or a P&L entry.
- **No true profit or labor modeling in the cheap tier:** Price lookups use national averages. Loaded labor cost (wage plus payroll tax plus drive time), overhead allocation and the margin per job appear only in the $99–$169 commercial tools.
- **Hard-coded formulas:** The best-selling calculator is explicitly not customizable, and some calculate only one add-on.
- **Canva-only "systems":** Hundreds of pages of design, but no logic, automation or data.
- **Filler template counts:** "180+", "300+" and "126 pages" include 70 Instagram posts, color variants and A4/Letter duplicates. PLR resale means the same content appears everywhere.
- **Tax framing:** Bookkeeping sheets are pitched as helping with "annual tax filings", which is implicitly US-centric. No country-neutral tax handling was seen.
- **No recurring-job engine:** Weekly, biweekly and monthly frequency is a dropdown in calculators, but nothing generates or tracks the actual recurring visits, skips or reschedules.
- **No QC:** Quality inspections appear only in SaaS (Swept Optimize at $150/mo). No template product seen offers inspection scoring tied to cleaners or clients.
- **Little documentation:** At best a "Read Me tab" or a PDF instruction sheet. No walkthrough videos were mentioned in the snippets for the under-$50 spreadsheet products.
- **Low review satisfaction on bloated bundles:** 2.0★ from 8 on a 300-page bundle (S?).

### 5.4 Missing functionality nobody provides well
1. **One connected pipeline from quote to job to invoice/income to P&L** in a spreadsheet, where a quote you accept becomes a scheduled job and completed jobs feed the books.
2. **Residential margin modeling with loaded labor.** The commercial tools do this, but no residential or maid-service product seen models cleaner hourly cost, payroll burden, drive time and supplies to show margin per job and per client.
3. **Recurring service management:** generating visits from frequency, tracking skips and cancellations, and forecasting recurring revenue.
4. **QC and inspection tracking** tied to cleaners and clients, with trends over time. Today this is only available in the $150/mo Swept tier.
5. **Profitability per client, per service type and per cleaner**, which would show which clients or services to reprice or drop.
6. **An AI layer:** no product seen bundles prompts for writing quotes, replying to reviews, handling complaints or creating marketing content. (Search found none; absence is not proof.)
7. **Real documentation and onboarding**, such as video walkthroughs and setup guides, in the spreadsheet tier.

### 5.5 How $100–$300 products justify their price
- **Margin protection and ROI framing (commercial calculators):** CleanBidsPro ($99) and QuickCal ($169) sell against the cost of underbidding one contract. They use loaded labor, overhead and risk math, and frame the purchase as one-time with no contract.
- **"Operating system" positioning (Notion $99–$199):** The pitch is to run everything in one place (clients, catalog, quotes, scheduling, staff, payments), implicitly replacing a subscription.
- **Education and authority bundling (CleanVantage $297, Bundly $199):** An ebook, roadmap, branding guide and business plan are packaged as a "launch system". The value is in the know-how, not the tooling.
- **Operator credibility and coaching adjacency:** Mad Clean's $99 bundle and Cleaning Company Blueprint's $250/hr coaching make templates the entry point to coaching.
- **Licensing:** Creative Market charges extra for a commercial license ($75), and PLR sellers charge for resell rights.
- **Bundle math:** "Build-a-bundle, save 15%" (RMMS) and "4 guides for $99 instead of $152" (Mad Clean) anchor against the à-la-carte total.

### 5.6 SaaS anchor vs a one-time spreadsheet OS
- **The anchor:** Jobber Core is $49/mo ($588/yr monthly-billed). Housecall Pro Basic is $59–79/mo. Launch27 Base is $75/mo. ZenMaid realistically costs $100–300/mo once cleaners are added. QuickBooks is another $38/mo. A 1–5 person cleaning business typically faces **$600–$2,300 per year in recurring software**.
- **Positioning:** A **$99–$199 one-time** connected spreadsheet OS costs about **2–4 months of a single SaaS subscription** and pays for itself within the first quarter. It is honestly positioned for **solo operators and teams of 1–5** who are pre-SaaS or SaaS-fatigued. It is **not** a replacement for online booking, automatic card charging, GPS clock-in or client SMS, and it should say so plainly. Suggested framing: "Run your numbers and operations for a year for less than 3 months of Jobber. Graduate to SaaS when you outgrow it, and export your data when you do."

## 6. Implications for our product

**Proposed product:** a spreadsheet-first, connected cleaning-business OS at ~$99–$199. It includes a quote-and-profit engine, CRM, jobs, recurring jobs, QC, an AI prompt library, a client document kit, a marketing kit and documentation.

1. **Lead with the connected engine, not the page count.** The market is flooded with 180–300 page Canva bundles for $10–$20 and PLR. Competing on page count is a race to the bottom, and the review signals suggest it disappoints buyers. Our headline should be **"quote → job → recurring schedule → QC → profit, all connected"**. Nothing under $200 does this today.
2. **The quote-and-profit engine is the wedge.** Bring commercial-grade math (loaded labor including payroll burden and drive time, supplies per visit, overhead allocation, target margin) to **residential, maid-service and Airbnb turnover** pricing. Show margin per quote before it is sent. This matches what justifies $99–$169 in the commercial tools, and it fixes the biggest weakness of the best-selling Etsy calculator: hard-coded national averages. Make every rate editable.
3. **Recurring jobs and QC are clear differentiators.** Generate visits from frequency and track skips and reschedules. Add a simple inspection score per job, rolled up by cleaner and client. QC is currently paywalled at about $150/mo in Swept.
4. **Profitability views:** margin per client, per service type and per cleaner, plus a "reprice these clients" list. No competitor seen offers this.
5. **Price placement:**
   - **$99 core** (the spreadsheet OS plus documentation) sits at the Notion-OS midpoint and matches CleanBidsPro.
   - **$149–$199 "complete"** adds the AI prompt library, client document kit, marketing kit and video walkthroughs. This matches the top Notion OSes and QuickCal, and stays under the $297 education bundles.
   - An optional commercial or reseller license should be a separate SKU, following the Creative Market pattern.
6. **Position against SaaS with honesty.** Show a side-by-side: one-time $149 vs Jobber $588+/yr, ZenMaid ~$1,200–3,600/yr for a small team, and QuickBooks $456/yr. State clearly what we don't do (online booking, auto-charge, GPS, SMS), and describe the business stage where SaaS becomes worth it.
7. **Documentation is a real advantage.** The spreadsheet tier offers at most a Read Me tab. Setup video walkthroughs, a quick-start with sample data, and a "first 30 minutes" guide are cheap to make and justify the premium over $5–$17 sheets.
8. **The AI prompt library is unclaimed territory.** No listing found bundles AI prompts for quotes, complaint replies, review responses, job-posting ads or social content specific to cleaning. It should be a named module, not filler.
9. **Keep the document and marketing kits lean and high-quality.** Fewer, better templates with a clear use for each, plus a note that contracts are **samples and not legal advice**. Avoid US-only tax claims in bookkeeping. Keep tax rate and currency as editable settings.
10. **Credibility and reviews matter as much as features.** The top seller wins on trust (5.0★, 342 shop reviews, 4,400+ sales). Plan for demo videos, real-looking sample data, a free "lite" calculator or CRM as a lead magnet (as Notion creators do), and early review collection.
11. **Distribution:** Etsy works for discovery, but its market sits below $20 and it discounts heavily. Our own site, Gumroad or Payhip should carry the $99–$199 offer. An Etsy listing could hold a cheap standalone calculator that leads buyers to the full OS.
12. **Before launch, verify live on the listing pages:** Clean With Carly's listing price, the Notion OS prices ($99/$199/Command Center), the Creative Market personal-license price, and the SaaS vendor pricing pages. This research could not open any of those pages directly.
