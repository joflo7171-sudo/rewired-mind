## Product description

Most cleaning-business templates give you a pile of separate files: a client list here, a price calculator there, a P&L sheet somewhere else. None of them talk to each other, and none of them tell you whether a job actually made money.

**Cleaning Business AI Growth OS is one connected workbook.** A lead becomes a quote, then a booked job, then a recurring plan, then a payment, and every step feeds your dashboard automatically.

**Know your profit before you send the quote.** Enter square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms, cleaning type, condition, frequency, crew size and add-ons. The Quote Builder shows the price, labor cost, supplies, travel, payment fees, gross profit, margin, revenue per labor hour, break-even price and the price you'd need to hit your target margin. Every assumption is yours to edit.

**Stop losing recurring revenue.** Recurring plans show when each visit is due, flag visits that haven't been booked, and total your recurring monthly value.

**See where you make and lose money.** Profit by service type, by cleaner and by customer type, with jobs below your target margin flagged automatically.

**Write faster with AI, safely.** 18 copy-and-paste AI workflows for lead replies, quote drafts, follow-ups, review requests and replies, referral asks, complaint responses, commercial proposals and weekly marketing ideas. They work with any general AI assistant, and there's nothing extra to buy.

**Look professional from day one.** 14 editable client forms and a lean marketing kit: flyer, door hanger, referral and review cards, and social and Google Business Profile templates.

One-time purchase. No subscription. Works in Microsoft Excel (2019 or newer / Microsoft 365) and Google Sheets.

## Ideal customer
- Owner-operators and small teams (1–10 cleaners) doing residential, move-in/out, short-term rental or light commercial cleaning
- Owners currently using notes, texts and scattered spreadsheets who want structure without a monthly software bill
- Owners who suspect some jobs or recurring clients are underpriced and want to see the real margin

**Not ideal for:** businesses that need online booking, automatic card charging, client text reminders, GPS clock-in or a mobile app. Those need scheduling software. This system can sit alongside it or come before it.

## FAQ
**Do I need Excel?** Excel 2019 or newer, Microsoft 365, or free Google Sheets. Apple Numbers isn't supported.

**Is it hard to set up?** Most owners can set up the basics in about 30 minutes with the Quick Start Guide. The DEMO file shows every feature working before you enter your own data.

**Does it tell me what to charge?** It calculates prices from your own assumptions (pay rates, speed, supplies, travel, target margin) so you can see time, cost and profit before you quote. It doesn't know your local market, and the example numbers included are starting points, not recommendations.

**Does it handle taxes or payroll?** No. There's an optional sales-tax field you control, but it is not accounting, tax or payroll software. Share your revenue and expense logs with your accountant.

**Do I need to pay for AI?** No. The workflows are prompts you copy into any general AI assistant, including free versions.

**Are the contracts and policies legally ready?** They're customizable templates. Have anything you rely on legally reviewed by a qualified professional where you operate.

**Can my team use it?** Yes, through Google Sheets sharing or Excel on OneDrive/SharePoint.

**Is this a subscription?** No. It's a one-time purchase.
