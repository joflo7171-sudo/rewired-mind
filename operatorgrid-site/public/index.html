The problem

Quote & Profit Engine

Know your profit before you send the quote.

Most cleaning quote calculators give you a price. This one shows what the job will cost you and what you'll actually keep, so you can stop underpricing work.

  • Your numbers, not national averages. Billing rates, cleaning speed, minutes per room, supplies, vehicle cost, fees and target margin are all editable.
  • True labor cost. Labor hours × pay rate, plus your own extra labor cost % and paid travel time.
  • Break-even and target price. See the lowest price that covers costs, and the price you'd need to hit your target margin.
  • Margin check. Quotes below break-even or below target are flagged before they go out.
  • Recurring value. See the monthly value of weekly, biweekly and monthly plans at a glance.

Is it right for you?

Built for owner-operators and small teams

This is a spreadsheet system, not a cleaning business software subscription. Here's an honest view of who it fits.

A good fit if you…

  • Run a residential, move-in/move-out, short-term rental or light commercial cleaning business
  • Work alone or with a small team of cleaners
  • Run things from texts, notes and scattered spreadsheets today
  • Want a cleaning business software alternative without a monthly bill
  • Suspect some jobs or recurring clients are underpriced and want to see the real margin
  • Are comfortable working in Excel or Google Sheets

Not the right tool if you need…

  • Live online booking for customers
  • Card processing or automatic payment collection
  • GPS clock-in or a mobile app for cleaners
  • Automated SMS or email reminders
  • Payroll
  • Full field-service software for a large operation

For those, a dedicated subscription platform is the better choice. This system can come before it or sit alongside it.

It calculates prices from your own assumptions (pay rates, speed, supplies, travel, target margin) so you can see time, cost and profit before you quote. It doesn't know your local market, and the included numbers are examples, not recommendations.
