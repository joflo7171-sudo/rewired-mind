# The Offer: Estimate Desk

## Ideal customer (test this, don't assume it)
Independent **residential HVAC** company, owner-led, ~3–15 technicians, sends replacement/repair estimates, uses Jobber, Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan (so estimate status is trackable), has no dedicated person chasing open estimates. Second-choice niches after the pilot: plumbing, roofing.

## The expensive problem
Estimates go out, the customer says "let me think about it," and no one follows up in a structured way. Each estimate already cost lead-gen money (a vendor-reported ~$70–$105 per lead, row 5 of the evidence table) plus a technician's trip.

## Exact service
1. **Backlog sweep (week 1):** export every open/unsold estimate from the last 6–12 months. Segment by age and value.
2. **Human follow-up cadence:** personal, owner-approved messages by email and live phone calls from the company's name and number, on the contractor's authorization. No bulk robocalls. Text only where the contractor can show prior consent (see compliance notes).
3. **Re-quote support:** book re-visits, answer financing/timing objections using the contractor's own offers; hand warm customers back to the contractor's staff to close.
4. **Weekly report:** estimates touched, replies, appointments, jobs won and dollar value, from the contractor's own system export.

We do **not** sell AI, do pricing, give legal/license advice, or promise results.

## Low-risk pilot (what they buy first)
**30-day Estimate Recovery Sprint**
- **$750 fixed** + **5% of the value of any job approved and paid within 60 days of our first touch**, only for estimates that were open 14+ days when we started.
- Contractor can stop any time with 7 days' notice; fixed fee non-refundable only for work already performed (pro-rate otherwise).
- Rationale: the fixed fee covers delivery cost (model: ~$310 cash margin in base case, about –$90 in conservative); the commission is where value is shared.

## Defensible starting price (and what supports it)
Pilot $750 and retainer **$900/mo + 5% commission**. Observed vendor prices: $297–$599/mo for follow-up/reactivation products and agency-lite plans; $1,500+ for marketing retainers. Our price sits above software because a human does the work. **Whether owners accept it is a test, not a fact.**

## Ongoing retainer (after a successful pilot)
**$900/mo + 5% commission** on attributed closed-and-paid jobs, covering follow-up on every new estimate within 48 hours and a standing cadence. Month-to-month after the first 60 days.

## Customer success criteria (agreed in writing before start)
- Appointments or conversations generated from previously dead estimates.
- Dollar value of jobs approved after first touch, **from their own system**.
- Target: pilot revenue attributed ≥ 3× the pilot fixed fee. This is a goal, not a guarantee.
- Walk-away rule: if the pilot produces zero replies after the full cadence, we say so and refund the fixed fee in full (a guarantee we can actually keep).

## Delivery process and cost (model assumptions)
Intake call (30 min) → CRM export + written authorization → owner approves message templates → we run cadence (about 16–24 delivery hours a month per client) → weekly report → month-end attribution review. Estimated direct cost ≈ $350–$670 per client per month at a $22–$28/hr assumed coordinator cost (see `estimate-desk-financial-model.xlsx`).

## Proof without exaggeration
- Only report numbers exported from the client's system, with screenshots/CSV kept with their permission.
- Any case study uses the client's own wording, their written approval, and states the sample size (the first pilot = n=1).
- Never publish "recovered $X" without showing how many estimates, over what period, and what we cannot attribute.

## Proposal / agreement skeleton (have an attorney review before use)
1. Parties, scope, term (30 days; then month-to-month).
2. Fees: $750 pilot; commission 5% on attributed jobs paid within 60 days; invoiced monthly.
3. Attribution rule: a job counts if the estimate was open ≥14 days at start, was touched by us, and was approved after the first touch, per the client's system of record. Disputed jobs: client shows contact records; split 50/50 if unresolved.
4. Client authorizes us to contact its own customers in its name; client represents it has a lawful basis/consent for each channel used; no messaging to numbers on its internal opt-out list.
5. Data: we use exports only to deliver the service, delete on termination within 30 days.
6. No guarantee of revenue. Full refund of the fixed fee if zero customer replies after a complete cadence.
7. Either party may terminate on 7 days' notice.
