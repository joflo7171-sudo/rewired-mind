## Validation checklist (the idea is NOT validated until the last box)
Stage 1 — Problem (discovery calls):
- [ ] ≥ 8 owners interviewed; ≥ 5 admit no structured estimate follow-up
- [ ] ≥ 5 can estimate open-quote count and average job value (record real numbers → replace model placeholders)
- [ ] ≥ 3 say $750 + 5% is acceptable or counter within ±30%

Stage 2 — Willingness to pay:
- [ ] ≥ 1 signed agreement
- [ ] ≥ 1 **pilot fee actually paid** (cash received)

Stage 3 — Value delivered:
- [ ] Backlog worked, cadence completed, weekly reports sent
- [ ] Attributed jobs counted from the client's own system export
- [ ] Share of estimates closing after first touch measured (model default 8%; below ~3% = reconsider)
- [ ] Client states in writing it would continue

Stage 4 — Repeatable:
- [ ] Pilot converts to retainer at ≥ 1 client
- [ ] Second and third pilots paid, delivery ≤ 24 hrs/client/month
- [ ] 3 clients active for 60+ days

**Kill/pivot rules:** after 40 contacts and 8 meetings with zero willingness to pay → reject B, test Candidate C (invoice follow-up). If pilots close < 3% of estimates after follow-up → B has no product; don't scale.

## Scaling (only after Stage 4)
Standardize cadence into an SOP; hire one part-time coordinator at ~5 clients; add weekly QA; move to retainer contracts; consider adding missed-call intake only as a feature (don't sell it alone); only then consider software tooling or a second metro.

**Three different "million" goals:** a million in *cumulative sales* needs roughly $1M ÷ $1,684 ≈ 600 client-months (about 50 clients for a year); a million in *profit* is ~3–4× more revenue at ~60% margins; a *company valued at $1M* depends on buyer multiples I can't verify and on how little it depends on you. None is inevitable.
