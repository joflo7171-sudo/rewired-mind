# Rewired Mind: Shorts Batch 1 (14 scripts)

Format: 20-35 sec, talk-to-camera or text-on-screen over existing cinematic b-roll style.
Every Short: hook in the first 2 seconds, one idea, CTA = "Take the free confidence quiz, link in bio."
Caption template: `[hook]. Free quiz -> rewiredmind.org #overthinking #confidence #anxietyrelief #mindset`
Repurpose each to YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels. Pin comment: "Free quiz to find your confidence blocker: rewiredmind.org"
Already produced in the repo: "stop overexplaining", "your brain is lying about discomfort". Skip those.

| # | Hook (first 2 sec) | Idea / payoff |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "You replay conversations at 2 a.m. for a reason." | Loop feels like problem-solving but has no next step. Fix: ask "what is the next action?" |
| 2 | "Stop saying 'I'll just overthink it less.'" | Forcing thoughts away backfires; give your mind a task instead. |
| 3 | "Why you rehearse texts 10 times before sending." | Seeking certainty to avoid disapproval. Send at draft 2 as practice. |
| 4 | "Confident people aren't less anxious. They do this." | They act before the loop finishes: the 5-second rule variant. |
| 5 | "You don't need more motivation. You need less negotiating." | Decide once in advance (if-then plan). |
| 6 | "If you stay quiet in meetings, read this." | Speak first within 5 minutes of a meeting starting. |
| 7 | "The apology you don't need to send." | Over-apologizing as a confidence leak; replace with "thanks for waiting." |
| 8 | "What 'I'm fine' costs you." | Suppressing vs naming; name the feeling in 3 words. |
| 9 | "Your inner critic is a bad coach. Here's the swap." | Rewrite the critic line into a coaching line. |
| 10 | "Why you can't decide, and how to fix it in 60 seconds." | Decision fatigue: set a time limit and a default. |
| 11 | "3 signs you're stuck in a mental loop." | Repetition, no new info, no next step. |
| 12 | "One question that breaks overthinking." | "What would I do if I trusted myself right now?" |
| 13 | "Hesitation is a habit, not a personality." | Ties to the quiz archetypes (Rising/Builder). |
| 14 | "I took a confidence quiz. Here's what it said." | Walk through the 4 results (Awakened, Builder, Rising, Warrior). Direct quiz CTA. |

## Posting plan
Week 1-2: 1 per day, same time. After 14 posts, look at views, average watch time, profile-link clicks, quiz starts. Make more of the top 3 formats and drop the bottom 3.
Do not claim clinical outcomes. Keep language educational.
