# Rewired Mind — Legitimate Backlink Prospects (Sept 2026)

Status: **research only — nothing has been sent.** Every pitch below needs Jonathan's approval before it goes out.

Rules followed: no paid links, no link farms/PBNs, no directory blasts, no comment spam, no reciprocal-link swaps, no bulk services. Prospects that turned out to require payment are listed at the bottom as **excluded**.

Research limits: the Rewired Mind site, Lifehack, Feedspot and several prospect pages were blocked from this research environment, so guidelines were confirmed through search snippets rather than by reading every page. Check each prospect's page before pitching. The live query feeds on HARO/Featured/Qwoted need a logged-in account, so no specific open journalist requests are listed. The platforms to sign up for are ranked instead.

## Journalist-source platforms — verdict

| Platform | Cost to sources | Fit | Do it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Featured.com / HARO** | Free | Best for consumer and lifestyle stories. Highest tier-1 approval rate reported (~18–20%). | **Yes. Start here.** |
| **Source of Sources** | Free | Consumer and lifestyle, but lower volume in 2026 | Yes. Skim it and move on. |
| **Qwoted** | Free (2 pitches/mo); Pro $99–149/mo | Good outlets, lower approval (~6%) | Free tier only. Don't pay yet. |
| **SourceBottle** | Mostly AU/NZ | Regional | Low priority unless targeting AU/NZ press |

Routine: 15 minutes a day, a maximum of 3 answers a day, only on topics you can answer credibly. Keep a saved bio line and headshot ready. Track every placement in a simple sheet (outlet, date, URL, link yes/no).

## Excluded (payment required — against the rules)

- **Addicted2Success** — offers "article packages" (paid placement)
- **YourTango Experts** — paid membership to publish
- **Reedsy Discovery** — paid submission fee
- **Good Men Project Guest Posting Membership** and **Psychreg sponsored posts** — only the free editorial routes above are acceptable

## Recommended first week (after approval)

1. Create free Featured/HARO, SOS and Qwoted source profiles, then start answering relevant questions daily (#1, #5, #6).
2. Write one personal essay and pitch it to Tiny Buddha (#2). Adapt the core idea into separate originals for Pick the Brain (#3) and The Good Men Project (#8). **Never submit the same article to more than one site.**
3. Send one podcast pitch to Jonny Pardoe (#4) and set up a free PodMatch profile (#15).

Drafts of each pitch can be prepared for review. None will be sent without sign-off.
